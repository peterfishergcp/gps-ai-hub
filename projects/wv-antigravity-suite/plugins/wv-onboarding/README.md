# WV Onboarding, Daily Brief & Digital Twin (`wv-onboarding`)

Day-1 productivity and onboarding skills for State of West Virginia staff using **Google Antigravity** alongside their **Gemini Enterprise (`GE`)** subscription.

## Included Skills
- **`/wv-onboard`** — 60-second interactive setup for your agency role, Digital Twin communication preferences (`wv-twin-profile.md`), and Gemini Enterprise instance handoff, plus 30-60-90 day onboarding & SOP playbooks.
- **`/model-guide`** — Practical guide to switching models in Antigravity Desktop, IDE, and CLI (`agy`)—including **Anthropic Claude (`Claude Sonnet` & `Claude Opus`)** alongside **Google Gemini**, Thinking/Reasoning effort levels, Dual-Model Cross-Reviews, Vertex AI Model Garden IT enablement, and state quota/compliance guardrails.
- **`/daily-brief`** — Morning priorities, calendar prep, meeting action items, and weekly status rollups grounded in your federated GE connectors (Outlook/Gmail, SharePoint/Drive, Teams) or pasted notes.
- **`/digital-twin`** — Draft replies, formal letters, constituent responses, and plain-language rewrites in your personal voice (strictly draft-only; never auto-sends).
- **`/inbox-triage`** — Rule-driven email and incident report triage separating immediate leadership escalations from routine items, with a fail-safe hold on unreadable attachments.
- **`/wv-update`** — Fast Git / HTTPS ZIP updater to pull newly published West Virginia skills and templates in under 2 seconds.
