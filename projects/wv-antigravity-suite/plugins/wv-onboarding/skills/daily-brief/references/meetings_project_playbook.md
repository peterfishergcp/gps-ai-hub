# Public Sector Meetings, Action Tracking & Project Delivery Playbook

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #2: Meetings & Scheduling*, *Playbook #8: Project & Program Management*, and *Playbook #12: Leadership & Team Management*).

## 1. Single-Meeting Prep Brief (`1 Page, 3-Minute Read`)
- **Sections:**
  1. **Meeting Purpose & Desired Outcome** (what needs to be decided before the call ends)
  2. **Attendee Context & Recent Touchpoints** (grounded in federated Outlook/Gmail/Teams/SharePoint via `ge_ask_instance` or pasted threads)
  3. **3 Key Talking Points & Anticipated Questions**
  4. **Open Commitments / Risks to Raise**

## 2. Outcome-Driven Agenda Builder
- Structures every agenda item as a **Question to Answer** or **Decision to Make**, with timeboxes (`5m`, `15m`), owner, pre-read link, and explicit `[Inform | Discuss | Decide]` tag.

## 3. Rough Notes / Transcript -> Action Items & Recap
- Extracts a clean **Decision Log** + **Action Item Table** (`Owner | Specific Action | Due Date | Source Quote/Timestamp`) + ready-to-review `[Draft: Recap Email]`. Never invent owners or dates—mark unassigned items `[OWNER NEEDED]`.

## 4. One-Page Project Charter & Milestone Plan
- **Sections:** Problem & Public Benefit, Scope (`In Scope` vs. `Explicitly Out of Scope`), Milestones & Target Dates, RACI Roles, Dependencies, and Success Metrics.

## 5. RAID Log (Risks, Assumptions, Issues & Dependencies)
- Builds a sortable Markdown/CSV table (`ID | Type | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / Next Step | Owner | Target Date`) ready to feed directly into `/wv-dashboard`.

## 6. Weekly Executive Status Report (`Green / Amber / Red`)
- **Format:** Overall RAG Status + 1-sentence headline -> **Accomplished This Week** -> **Planned Next Week** -> **Blockers / Decisions Needed from Leadership** (with options and recommended path).
