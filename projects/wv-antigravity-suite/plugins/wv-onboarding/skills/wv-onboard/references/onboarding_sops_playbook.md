# Public Sector Hiring, Onboarding & Institutional SOP Playbook

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #6: SOPs & Institutional Knowledge* and *Playbook #7: Hiring, Onboarding & Workforce*). Load and apply the matching template whenever a user asks to onboard staff, document processes, or preserve institutional knowledge.

## 1. New Hire 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan
- **Inputs:** Role title, division/program, key systems (e.g., Gemini Enterprise, Antigravity, state ERP/grant portal), first-quarter deliverables.
- **Output Sections:**
  1. **Days 1–30 (Learn & Connect):** Mandatory access/security setup, `/wv-onboard` + `/connect-ge` setup, stakeholder listening tour, core statutory/program reading list, 1 early quick-win deliverable.
  2. **Days 31–60 (Contribute & Co-Own):** Independent ownership of recurring workflows, shadowing FOIA/procurement/reporting cycles, mid-point check-in rubric with supervisor.
  3. **Days 61–90 (Lead & Improve):** Full operational cadence, process improvement recommendation, 90-day success milestones.

## 2. Onboarding Welcome Packet & System Access Checklist
- Produces a welcoming Day-1 guide: mission overview, org chart placeholders, acronym glossary, IT/security checklist, communication norms (when to use Email vs. Teams/Chat vs. formal memo), and first-week schedule.

## 3. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) From Rough Notes
- Converts rough bullet notes, recorded walkthrough transcripts, or legacy emails into a numbered, audit-ready SOP:
  - **Header:** SOP ID, Effective Date, Process Owner, Review Cadence, Governing Policy/Statute.
  - **Prerequisites & Access Roles** -> **Step-by-Step Procedure** (one action per numbered step, expected outcome per step) -> **Exception & Escalation Handling** -> **Quality Verification Checklist**.

## 4. Continuity Desk Guide ("Retiring / Transitioning Expert Playbook")
- Interviews a departing or transitioning subject-matter expert (or synthesizes their SharePoint/Drive folder via Gemini Enterprise `StreamAssist`) into a structured **Continuity Desk Guide**:
  - Annual/quarterly statutory calendar ("things that only happen once a year"), key external contacts, recurring gotchas, file/data locations, and top 10 FAQ scenarios.

## 5. Plain-Language Position Description & Structured Interview Guide
- Translates dense civil-service classifications into clear, skills-based job postings and consistent, job-related behavioral interview scorecards (`[VERIFY WITH AGENCY HR BEFORE POSTING]`).
