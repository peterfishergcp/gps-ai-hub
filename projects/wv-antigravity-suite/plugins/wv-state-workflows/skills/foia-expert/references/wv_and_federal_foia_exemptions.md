# West Virginia FOIA (`W. Va. Code § 29B-1-1 et seq.`) & Federal FOIA (`5 U.S.C. § 552`) Quick Reference

> **Legal Disclaimer:** Reference guide for drafting Vaughn Index justifications prior to mandatory review by agency legal counsel / Office of the Attorney General.

## Statutory Timeline (`W. Va. Code § 29B-1-3(d)`)
- **5 Business Days:** Public body custodian must respond within **five working days** (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal public holidays) by either:
  1. Furnishing copies of the requested public records,
  2. Advising the requester in writing of the time and place they may inspect and copy the records, OR
  3. Denying the request in whole or in part in writing, stating the specific statutory exemption(s) and notifying the requester of their right to institute proceedings in circuit court (`W. Va. Code § 29B-1-5`).

## Core Statutory Exemptions Crosswalk

| Exemption Category | West Virginia FOIA Citation | Federal FOIA Analog | Typical Redacted / Withheld Items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trade Secrets & Commercial/Financial** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(1)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(4)` | Vendor bank/ABA routing numbers, proprietary pricing models, unpatented technical designs submitted in confidence. |
| **Personal Privacy (PII / Personnel / Medical)** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(2)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(6)` | SSNs, DOBs, residential addresses, personal phone/email, medical/benefit files, non-public personnel evaluations where disclosure constitutes an unreasonable invasion of privacy. |
| **Law Enforcement & Investigation Records** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(4)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(7)` | Active law-enforcement / regulatory investigative files, confidential informant identities, tactical procedures. |
| **Statutory Confidentiality Cross-References** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(5)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(3)` | Records specifically exempted from disclosure by another state or federal statute (e.g., HIPAA, FERPA, CJIS, tax returns). |
| **Internal Pre-Decisional Memoranda & Attorney-Client** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(8)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(5)` | Internal agency memoranda/letters reflecting pre-decisional deliberative advice or privileged attorney-client work product (*segregate and release purely factual material!*). |
| **Cybersecurity, Infrastructure & Vulnerability Data** | `W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4(a)(9)` & `(a)(19)` | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(7)(E)` | Security plans, network vulnerability assessments, cryptographic keys, security codes, critical infrastructure protection details. |
