# Public Sector Policy, Finance/Budget & IT Operations Playbook

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #3: Policy & Legislative Analysis*, *Playbook #10: Finance, Budget & Audit*, and *Playbook #11: IT & Digital Services*).

## Policy & Legislative Recipes
1. **Cross-Jurisdiction Policy Comparison:** Compares how 3–5 peer states (especially neighboring Appalachian/mid-Atlantic states) structure a program, statute, or fee schedule (`State | Statutory Authority | Funding Mechanism | Eligibility | Lessons Learned`).
2. **Legislative Hearing & Testimony Prep:** Drafts a crisp 3-minute opening testimony (`~425 words`) plus a **Tough Q&A Prep Pack** anticipating fiscal, constituent-impact, and implementation timeline questions.
3. **Pre-Publication Fact & Citation Audit:** Scans any draft memo or report for unsupported claims, arithmetic mismatches, undefined acronyms, or missing dates.

## Finance, Budget & Audit Recipes
4. **Budget Justification & Fiscal Note Narrative:** Translates line-item spreadsheets into a clear public-value narrative connecting requested FTEs/funding to measurable statutory outcomes—checking all subtotals deterministically.
5. **Budget-vs.-Actual Variance Explainer:** Flags line items exceeding variance thresholds (e.g. `+/- 10%`), separates timing shifts from structural overruns, and drafts corrective options.
6. **Audit Finding Corrective Action Plan (CAP):** Maps each internal/external audit finding to Root Cause, Specific Remediation Deliverable, Accountable Owner, and Verification Artifact.

## IT Operations & Service Delivery Recipes
7. **Plain-Language Incident Postmortem (Blameless):** Timeline of events, user impact, root cause, what went well, and concrete preventive engineering/process actions.
8. **Change Management & System Rollout Comms:** Translates technical cutover plans into clear, non-jargon "What's Changing & What You Need to Do" guides for agency staff.
