# Public Sector Procurement, SOW & Grants Management Playbook

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #5: Procurement, Contracting & Grants*).

## 1. Solicitation / RFP Requirements Matrix Extractor
- Parses a 50+ page federal NOFO or vendor RFP into a compliance traceability matrix: `Section/Page | Mandatory ("Shall/Must") vs. Desirable ("Should") | Requirement Summary | Evidence / Response Owner | Status`.

## 2. Outcome-Based Statement of Work (SOW) Drafter
- **Sections:** Background & Objective, Scope of Services, **Deliverables & Written Acceptance Criteria Table**, Period of Performance, Agency vs. Vendor Responsibilities, Security/Data Handling Requirements, Payment Schedule Tied to Accepted Milestones, and Key Personnel / Transition-Out Plan.

## 3. Vendor Proposal Side-by-Side Comparison (Objective Rubric)
- Evaluates multiple vendor responses strictly against published solicitation evaluation criteria—quoting exact vendor commitments, flagging vague *"roadmap / future"* answers vs. GA capabilities, and listing clarifying questions for oral presentations.

## 4. Federal / State Grant Application & Reporting Helper
- Structures **Statement of Need**, **Project Design & Milestones** (such as infrastructure / site readiness tiers), **Budget Narrative Alignment Check** (verifying line items sum accurately to the total ask), and **Quarterly Performance Progress Report** drafts.
