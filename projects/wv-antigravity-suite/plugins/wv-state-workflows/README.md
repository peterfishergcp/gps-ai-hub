# WV State Workflows — FOIA, Legal & Policy Review (`wv-state-workflows`)

Codifies high-stakes public-sector compliance, public records, procurement, and policy workflows for State of West Virginia divisions—requiring **zero extra software installs** on employee laptops.

## Included Skills
- **`/foia-expert`** — Adapted from `google-vault-foia-search-expert` and extended for West Virginia: translates narrative citizen FOIA requests into compliant **Google Vault** (`MAIL`, `DRIVE`, `GROUPS`, `CHAT`), **Microsoft Purview eDiscovery KQL** (Outlook, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams), and **Federated Gemini Enterprise (`GE`)** queries; applies segregable PII/trade-secret redactions under **`W. Va. Code § 29B-1-4`** and **`5 U.S.C. § 552(b)`**; and outputs a 4-part **FOIA Record Processing & Redaction Report** with a **Line-by-Line Vaughn Index** and print-ready sanitized deliverables gated for legal counsel review.
- **`/legal-procurement-review`** — Reviews vendor agreements, SOWs, RFPs, and grant terms against public-sector procurement safeguards (sovereign immunity, fiscal-year non-appropriation, indemnification, data ownership, AI model-training opt-outs) with a `1:3:9` risk scorecard.
- **`/policy-program-audit`** — Legislative/rule analysis, cross-jurisdiction comparisons, risk registers, and cross-document consistency checks before program launch.
