# 4-Tier Recency Compaction Rubric

Keep `initiatives/<slug>/running_notes.md` under ~300 lines even across year-long programs by compacting older notes rather than deleting history.

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Age Window | Target Length | What to Keep | What to Compress |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | `0-7 Days` | 8-15 lines per call | Exact numbers, stakeholder quotes/concerns, open blockers, and assigned next steps | Small talk, scheduling logistics, and repeated intros |
| **Tier 2** | `8-30 Days` | 3-5 bullets per call | Decisions made, scope changes, unresolved risks | Resolved tactical sub-steps already checked off on `action_items.md` |
| **Tier 3** | `31-90 Days` | 1 line per milestone | Dated milestone outcome (`YYYY-MM-DD: Completed Phase 1 UAT with 0 P0 defects`) | Per-meeting back-and-forth |
| **Tier 4** | `90+ Days` | 1 short paragraph | Original business problem, baseline metrics, and non-negotiable constraints | Superseded drafts and historical status updates |
