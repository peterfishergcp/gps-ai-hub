# Plain-Language Public & Constituent Notice Rewriter

Use this skill whenever the user asks to simplify, rewrite, or clarify an official notice, constituent letter, or operational bulletin for the public.

## Workflow & Rules

1. **PII Check First**: Flag any real constituent PII (names paired with case IDs, DOBs, SSNs, addresses) before drafting.
2. **Action-First Structure**:
   - Lead in the first two sentences with **what changed, when it takes effect, and the single most important action** the reader must take.
   - Write at a **6th-to-8th-grade reading level** in a respectful, direct tone with short sentences and zero unexplained acronyms.
   - Include clear subheadings including **What You Need to Do Next** and **Questions or Assistance**.
   - Mark any missing contact number, date, or URL inline as **`[NEEDS CONFIRMATION: detail]`**.
3. **Preserved Rules Audit List**:
   - At the bottom of the draft, append a short **Preserved Requirements Check** listing every deadline, ID requirement, or policy rule kept intact from the source text, followed by a human-review reminder.
