# Emergency Readiness & HSEEP Crisis Studio (`emergency-readiness`)

A standalone **Google Antigravity (`AGY`) Plugin** for State, County, Municipal, Tribal, and Federal Emergency Management teams—bundling two zero-dependency skills that generate 100% offline-capable single-file HTML applications:

1. **`/ttx-simulator` (`skills/ttx-simulator/`)** — **HSEEP Multi-Role Tabletop & Crisis Simulator**:
   - 6 **Fog-of-War** agency role seats (each hiding other agencies' private blind spots until Unified Command shares intelligence).
   - Live exercise clock, 16-unit dispatchable multi-agency resource roster, and 4 timed multi-domain injects with **Dynamic Consequence Branching** (`cascadeAlert` banners when earlier choices create downstream hazards).
   - Interactive **ERG 2024 SVG Plume & 4-Sheet Plotter Map**, live **`<= 120`-Word 7-Essentials Public Warning Auto-Scorer (`0–100`)**, and **90-Day FEMA EMPG After-Action Report / Improvement Plan (`AAR/IP`)** exporter.
2. **`/field-ops-kit` (`skills/field-ops-kit/`)** — **Field Readiness, Forensic Crosswalk & Regional SEOC Kit**:
   - **7-Tab Disaster Timeline Operational Binder** (`24` plain-language county tools with crimson Deobligation Trap warnings and copyable task-order riders).
   - **25-Question County Readiness Self-Check** across 5 pillars with live FEMA deobligation risk scoring and auto-sorted corrective action plans.
   - **6-Check Forensic Debris Hauling Record-Gap Crosswalk** with a live **Max Loaded-Trip Minutes Slider (`35–65 min`)** reconciling gate-camera SHA-256 photo hashes, registered empty truck tares, certified landfill scale tickets, and contractor invoices into a **Federal Reviewer Justification Memo** (`2 CFR § 200.403`).
   - **Multi-Region SEOC Crosswalk ("Big Map" Matrix)** cross-indexing counties, county seats, EMA areas, USACE districts, DOT districts, State Police troops, RPDCs, river basins, and unlisted local flood works.

Ships pre-loaded with **State of West Virginia (`55` counties)** flagship templates and supports **1-Prompt `"Swap to My State"`** customization via `stateMeta` for any US state or county.
