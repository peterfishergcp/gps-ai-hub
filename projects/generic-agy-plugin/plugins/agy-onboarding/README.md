# AGY Onboarding, Daily Brief & Digital Twin (`agy-onboarding`)

Day-1 productivity and onboarding skills for Public Sector staff using **Google Antigravity** alongside their **Gemini Enterprise (`GE`)** subscription and **Second Brain Memory Vault** (`~/antigravity-twin/memory/`).

## Included Skills
- **`/agy-onboard`** — 60-second interactive setup for your agency role, Digital Twin communication preferences (`agy-twin-profile.md`), optional 1-step **2-Tier Second Brain Vault** initialization (`public-sector` preset), and Gemini Enterprise instance handoff, plus 30-60-90 day onboarding & SOP playbooks.
- **`/model-guide`** — Practical guide to switching models in Antigravity Desktop, IDE, and CLI (`agy`)—including **Anthropic Claude (`Claude Sonnet` & `Claude Opus`)** alongside **Google Gemini**, Thinking/Reasoning effort levels, Dual-Model Cross-Reviews, Vertex AI Model Garden IT enablement, and state quota/compliance guardrails.
- **`/daily-brief`** — Unified morning kickoff brief, calendar prep, and weekly rollups combining your federated GE connectors (Outlook/Gmail, SharePoint/Drive, Teams) and Second Brain vault (`P0`/`P1` GTD commitments, active 4-file initiatives, and `>21d` stale stakeholder alerts).
- **`/digital-twin`** — Draft replies, decision memos, formal letters, constituent responses, and plain-language rewrites calibrated to `~/antigravity-twin/memory/profile/voice.md` and `agy-twin-profile.md` (strictly draft-only; pairs with `/no-ai-slop` and `/outbound-scrub`).
- **`/inbox-triage`** — Rule-driven email and incident report triage separating immediate leadership escalations from routine items, with a fail-safe hold on unreadable attachments.
- **`/agy-update`** — Fast Git / HTTPS SHA-256 updater to pull newly published Public Sector skills and sidecars in under 2 seconds.
