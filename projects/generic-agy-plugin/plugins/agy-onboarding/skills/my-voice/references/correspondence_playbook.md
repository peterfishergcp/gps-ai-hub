# Public Sector Correspondence, Constituent Services & Plain-Language Playbook

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #1: Email & Daily Communication* and *Playbook #4: Constituent & Stakeholder Communication*).

## 1. Draft a Reply in My Voice
- Match sentence length, greeting/sign-off style, and directness from `~/.gemini/config/plugin_data/my-antigravity/memory/profile/voice.md` (or 2–3 sample emails). Keep paragraphs to `<= 3` sentences. State the answer or ask in Sentence 1.

## 2. Formal Letter to an Official, Board, or Interagency Partner
- **Structure:** Official Date & Addressee Block -> Subject (`RE: ...`) -> Purpose & Statutory/Program Reference -> Clear Summary of Facts -> Specific Action/Coordination Requested & Timeline -> Point of Contact Block.

## 3. Constituent Inquiry & Casework Response Drafter
- **Principles:** Empathetic, respectful, zero bureaucratic jargon, ~8th-grade reading level.
- **Structure:**
  1. Acknowledge their specific question or situation directly.
  2. Give a clear answer: what happens next, exact steps the resident can take, required documents, and realistic processing timeframe.
  3. Provide verified phone/portal contact paths and appeal/help options if applicable (`[CONFIRM URL/PHONE BEFORE SENDING]`).

## 4. Tone Check & Plain-Language Rewrite
- Audits a draft for: passive voice, undefined acronyms, defensive or overly legalistic phrasing, buried action items, and unintended friction—returning a **Before vs. After** comparison and a 3-bullet rationale.

## 5. Graceful Decline, Scope Redirect, or Timeline Reset
- Preserves strong interagency/partner relationships while clearly stating what cannot be accommodated, why (policy, capacity, or statutory boundary), and offering a concrete alternative or revised date.
