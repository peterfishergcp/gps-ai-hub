# AGY Public Workflows — FOIA, Legal, Policy & Government Services

Public-sector domain workflows combining **3 core agency playbooks** with **7 Government Services (`public_sector`) skills**, all toggled cleanly inside **`Extensions -> AGY Skills`**:

## Public Workflows (3 Core Skills)
- **`/foia-expert`** — Translates narrative citizen public records requests into compliant **Google Vault** (`MAIL`, `DRIVE`, `GROUPS`, `CHAT`), **Microsoft Purview eDiscovery KQL** (Outlook, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams), and **Federated Gemini Enterprise (`GE`)** queries; applies segregable PII/trade-secret redactions under **`5 U.S.C. § 552(b)`** and applicable state public records acts; and outputs a 4-part **FOIA Record Processing & Redaction Report** with a **Line-by-Line Vaughn Index** and print-ready sanitized deliverables gated for legal counsel review.
- **`/legal-procurement-review`** — Pre-screens vendor contracts, SaaS/AI order forms, SOWs, and grant agreements against public-sector sovereign immunity, fiscal-year non-appropriation, one-way indemnification prohibitions, public records openness, data residency, and AI training opt-outs using `1:3:9` risk scoring.
- **`/policy-program-audit`** — Produces structured legislative/rule impact briefs and runs cross-document launch consistency audits catching conflicting dollar caps, dates, or eligibility rules across rules, flyers, and web pages.

## Government Services (`public_sector`) Skills (7 Skills)
- **`/document-intake-cleanup-and-validation`** — Constituent proof-document quality screening and deterministic prospective monthly gross income calculation.
- **`/expense-auditor`** — Subrecipient grant ledger auditing against **2 CFR Part 200 (Uniform Guidance)** and award terms.
- **`/foia-request-processing`** — First-pass FOIA search scoping and 3-table officer report (`Matched Documents`, `Review Items`, `Gaps`).
- **`/plain-language-notice-generator`** — Active-voice plain-language rewriting of regulatory advisories with zero fact invention.
- **`/policy-and-statute-assistant`** — Federal/state regulatory hierarchy research with strict anti-hallucination citation gates.
- **`/public-records-redaction`** — 50-state & federal first-pass public records redactions with pinpoint citations and officer flags.
- **`/rfp-vendor-evaluation`** — Page-cited RFP mandatory pass/fail matrices, contradiction flags, and scored criterion evidence.
