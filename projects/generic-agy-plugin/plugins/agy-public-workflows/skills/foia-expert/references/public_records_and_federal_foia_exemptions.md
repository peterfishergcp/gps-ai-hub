# Federal FOIA (`5 U.S.C. § 552`) & State Public Records Quick Reference

> **Legal Disclaimer:** Reference guide for drafting Vaughn Index justifications prior to mandatory review by agency legal counsel / Office of the Attorney General. Always confirm the exact governing state or local statute section before releasing records externally.

## Statutory Response Clocks (Confirm Governing Jurisdiction)
- **Federal FOIA (`5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A)`):** Agencies generally have **20 working days** (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal public holidays) to determine whether to comply with a request and notify the requester.
- **State & Local Public Records Acts:** Statutory response windows typically range from **3 to 10 business days** (excluding weekends and legal holidays) to:
  1. Furnish copies of the requested public records,
  2. Advise the requester in writing of the time and place they may inspect and copy the records (or provide a statutory extension notice for voluminous requests), OR
  3. Deny the request in whole or in part in writing, stating the specific statutory exemption(s) and informing the requester of administrative appeal or judicial review rights.

## Core Statutory Exemptions Crosswalk

| Exemption Category | Federal FOIA Citation | State Public Records Analog (Confirm Local Section) | Typical Redacted / Withheld Items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trade Secrets & Commercial/Financial** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(4)` | State Trade Secrets / Confidential Commercial Info Exemption | Vendor bank/ABA routing numbers, proprietary pricing models, unpatented technical designs submitted in confidence. |
| **Personal Privacy (PII / Personnel / Medical)** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(6)` | State Personal Privacy / Unwarranted Invasion Exemption | SSNs, DOBs, residential addresses, personal phone/email, medical/benefit files, non-public personnel evaluations where disclosure constitutes an unreasonable invasion of privacy. |
| **Law Enforcement & Investigation Records** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(7)` | State Law Enforcement / Investigative Records Exemption | Active law-enforcement / regulatory investigative files, confidential informant identities, tactical procedures. |
| **Statutory Confidentiality Cross-References** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(3)` | State "Exempted by Other Law" Provision | Records specifically exempted from disclosure by another state or federal statute (e.g., `26 U.S.C. § 6103` FTI, `20 U.S.C. § 1232g` FERPA, `28 U.S.C. § 534` CJIS, tax returns). |
| **Internal Pre-Decisional Memoranda & Attorney-Client** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(5)` | State Deliberative Process & Attorney-Client Privilege Exemption | Internal agency memoranda/letters reflecting pre-decisional deliberative advice or privileged attorney-client work product (*segregate and release purely factual material!*). |
| **Cybersecurity, Infrastructure & Vulnerability Data** | `5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(7)(E)` | State Critical Infrastructure & Cybersecurity Exemption | Security plans, network vulnerability assessments, cryptographic keys, internal admin IPs, security codes, critical infrastructure protection details. |
