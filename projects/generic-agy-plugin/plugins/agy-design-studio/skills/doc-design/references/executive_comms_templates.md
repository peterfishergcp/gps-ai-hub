# Public Sector Executive Docs, Data Storytelling & External Comms Templates

Adapted from the **GPS Public Sector Skills Library** (*Playbook #3: Policy & Executive Briefings*, *Playbook #6: Data & Reporting Narratives*, and *Playbook #9: Communications & Public Affairs*).

## 1. One-Page Decision Memo (`BLUF`)
- **TO / FROM / DATE / SUBJECT / DECISION NEEDED BY:** `<Date>`
- **Bottom Line Recommendation:** 2-sentence actionable recommendation.
- **Why Now (Context & Authority):** Statutory, budget, or operational driver.
- **Options Evaluated:** `Option A (Status Quo)` vs. `Option B (Recommended)` vs. `Option C` across Cost, Staffing, Risk, and Public Impact.
- **Implementation Next Steps:** First 3 milestones upon approval.

## 2. 2-Page Program & Grant Stakeholder Flyer (`HTML Print-to-PDF`)
- **Page 1 (Overview & Value):** Hero banner (`#053254`), Total Funding / Key Dates strip (`#C1452C` & `#D5A245`), Program Tracks side-by-side comparison cards, Who Can Apply.
- **Page 2 (Process, Checklist & Contact):** Step-by-step application timeline, required documentation checklist, scoring/priority criteria, and agency point-of-contact footer.

## 3. Data-to-Narrative Executive Brief ("What the Numbers Mean")
- Pairs well with `/agy-dashboard`: translates raw CSV/BigQuery tables into (1) **Headline Trend**, (2) **3 Notable Outliers or County/Division Shifts**, (3) **Data Limitations / Caveats**, and (4) **Policy or Operational Implication**.

## 4. Press Release, Talking Points & Plain-Language Public Notice
- **Press Release:** Embargo/Release Header -> Headline & Subhead -> Dateline (`CAPITOL CITY, STATE`) -> Lead paragraph (`Who, What, Why It Matters to Residents`) -> Leadership Quote Placeholder (`[APPROVE QUOTE]`) -> Program Details -> Boilerplate & Media Contact.
- **Public Notice / Resident FAQ:** Short Q&A headings (*"Who qualifies?"*, *"What is the deadline?"*, *"What documents do I need?"*, *"How do I get help?"*).
